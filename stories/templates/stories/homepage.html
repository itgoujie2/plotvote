Frequently Asked Questions About AI Storytelling

What is crowdsourced storytelling?

Crowdsourced storytelling is a collaborative approach to fiction writing where a community of readers and writers work together to create and shape narratives. On PlotVote, readers vote on story directions, submit plot ideas, and influence how AI-generated chapters unfold, making every participant a co-author.

How does AI storytelling work on PlotVote?

Our AI storytelling system uses advanced language models to generate complete story chapters based on community-selected prompts. After a week of voting, the winning prompt is transformed into a professionally-written chapter that continues the narrative while maintaining consistency with previous chapters.

What makes PlotVote different from other interactive fiction platforms?

Unlike traditional interactive fiction with pre-written paths, PlotVote combines collaborative writing with AI generation for unlimited storytelling possibilities. Stories evolve organically through community votes rather than following predetermined branches, creating truly unique narratives that no single author could create alone.

Is PlotVote free to use?

Yes! PlotVote is completely free for reading stories, voting on prompts, and participating in our crowdsourced storytelling community. You can create an account, vote on your favorite story directions, and watch AI-generated chapters unfold without any cost.

Can I submit my own story ideas?

Absolutely! You can pitch your story concepts to the community. If your pitch receives enough upvotes (typically 10), it becomes an active story where readers can submit and vote on chapter prompts. This collaborative fiction model ensures only the most engaging stories get developed.

How often are new chapters released?

New chapters are typically generated weekly after the voting period closes. This regular cadence keeps communities engaged and gives everyone time to read, vote, and submit prompts for upcoming chapters in their favorite collaborative stories.
