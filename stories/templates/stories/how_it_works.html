03

Submit & Vote on Plots

Shape the Story Through Democratic Voting

This is where crowdsourced storytelling comes alive. For each chapter, community members propose plot directions and vote on their favorites. Democracy determines the story's path.

A
Submit Plot Options

Propose what should happen next in the story. Describe character decisions, plot developments, or narrative directions. Be creative but consider story consistency.

B
Review Community Options

Read plot options submitted by other community members. Consider which direction would create the most engaging interactive fiction experience.

C
Cast Your Vote

Vote for your preferred plot direction. Each member gets one vote per chapter. The option with the most votes wins and guides AI generation.

Pro Tip: Good plot options balance creativity with narrative consistency. They advance the story meaningfully while respecting established characters and world-building.

04

AI Generates Chapter

Watch AI Transform Votes into Narrative

Once voting closes, our AI storytelling system generates a complete chapter based on the winning direction. This typically takes 30-90 seconds.

How AI Chapter Generation Works:
1
Context Analysis: AI reviews all previous chapters, character profiles, plot points, and story framework
2
Plot Integration: The winning plot option is incorporated into the narrative arc while maintaining consistency
3
Prose Generation: AI writes engaging prose matching the story's established tone, style, and genre conventions
4
Quality Check: The system ensures narrative coherence, character voice consistency, and proper pacing
5
Publication: The completed chapter is published for the community to read and enjoy

Technology: PlotVote uses GPT-4, one of the most advanced language models, to generate AI-generated stories with professional-quality prose, dialogue, and narrative structure.

Tips for Successful Collaborative Storytelling

Be Creative But Consistent

Propose innovative plot directions while respecting established characters, world-building, and tone. The best collaborative fiction balances creativity with continuity.

Engage with the Community

Participate in discussions, explain your plot proposals, and build consensus. Crowdsourced storytelling thrives on communication and collaborative spirit.

Be Patient and Persistent

Not every plot proposal will win votes—that's democracy! Stay engaged, learn from community preferences, and keep contributing. Great interactive fiction emerges from sustained participation.

Read Before You Vote

Review all previous chapters before voting on plot directions. Understanding the full story context leads to better decisions and higher-quality AI-generated stories.

Embrace the Unexpected

Collaborative storytelling produces surprises. Sometimes the community chooses unexpected directions—embrace these moments as opportunities for creative discovery.

Think Long-Term

Consider how plot options affect future chapters, character arcs, and story resolution. The best contributors to interactive fiction think strategically about narrative development.
